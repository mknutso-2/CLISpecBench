FIELD DEFINITION AND SCOPE

Projected date of publication used in bibliographic records for works that have not yet been published.

When field 263 is present, Leader/17 (Encoding level) contains value 8 (Prepublication level). Used for works that have not yet been published but which have been cataloged as part of a cataloging-in-publication (CIP) program or similar programs that create prepublication records, such as the U.S. ISSN national center program. Data is provided by any publisher participating in the program for items it plans to publish. Field 263 is maintained in the record until the work is published and the item is received by the program.

GUIDELINES FOR APPLYING CONTENT DESIGNATORS

■ INDICATORS

  • Both indicator positions are undefined; each contains a blank (#).

■ SUBFIELD CODES

$a - Projected publication date
Six-digit date recorded in the pattern yyyymm (4 digits for the year; 2 digits for the month). A hyphen (-) is used for an unknown portion of the date.
If the date covers more than one month, the number of the last month is recorded. Single digit months are right justified and unused positions contain a zero.
263 ##$a200011
[The item is to be published in November 2000.]
263 ##$a1999--
[The item is to be published in 1999; the scheduled month is not known.]
263 ##$a200102
[The item is to be published in Jan./Feb. 2001.]

INPUT CONVENTIONS

Representation of seasons - When the date is given in terms of a season of the year, the last month of the season is used and customarily recorded as follows:
Season Month (Northern Hemisphere) Month (Southern Hemisphere)
Winter 03 09
Spring 06 12
Summer 09 03
Fall 12 06

CONTENT DESIGNATOR HISTORY

Prior to 1999, the data in this field was recorded with only the last two digits of the year: yymm.
