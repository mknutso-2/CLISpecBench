FIELD DEFINITION AND SCOPE

Copy-specific field that contains information concerning the ownership and custodial history of the described materials from the time of their creation to the time of their accessioning, including the time at which individual items or groups of items were first brought together in their current arrangement or collation.

The immediate source of acquisition is recorded in field 541 (Immediate Source of Acquisition Note).

Ownership and custodial history in structured form, including links to an authority file, is recorded in field 361 (Structured Ownership and Custodial History).

This field can also be used to record: 1) the owner's name and address when an item is located other than at the owner's address (the current location of an item is recorded in field 852 (Location)); 2) the original site of a work when it was originally commissioned/installed at one site but was subsequently moved to another location (the current site is recorded in field 851); or 3) the last known owner/location when the work being described has been stolen or destroyed.

GUIDELINES FOR APPLYING CONTENT DESIGNATORS

■ INDICATORS

First Indicator - Privacy

Whether the contents of the field are private or not. The state of being private includes information that institutions do not want to display to the public because it is sensitive or restricted.

# - No information provided

No information is provided as to the privacy of the field contents.

$u - Uniform Resource Identifier

Uniform Resource Identifier (URI), for example a URL or URN, which provides electronic access data in a standard syntax. The data that is accessed by the URI contains additional information about ownership and custodial history.

INPUT CONVENTIONS

Punctuation - Field 561 ends with a period unless another mark of punctuation is present. If the final subfield is subfield $5, the mark of punctuation precedes that subfield.

CONTENT DESIGNATOR HISTORY

This field was defined in 1983 when the scope of field 541 was restricted to information about the immediate source of acquisition. Field 541 in records created prior to 1984 may contain provenance data.

Indicator 1 - Privacy [NEW, 2004]
$b - Time of collation (NR) [OBSOLETE, 1997]

Until subfield $a (History) was redefined in 1997 to include all details of ownership and custodial history, the date of described materials were brought together in their present form was recorded in subfield $b.

$u - Uniform Resource Identifier (R) [NEW, 2010]
