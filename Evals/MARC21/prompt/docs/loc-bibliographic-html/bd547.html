FIELD DEFINITION AND SCOPE

Description of the complex relationship between titles proper whenever an intelligible note cannot be system generated from the data in field 247 (Former Title).

Field 547 is used on latest entry and integrated entry catalog records (Continuing resources 008/34 field, codes 1 and 2, respectively); it is not used in successive entry cataloging. Former titles proper may also be recorded in field 247 (Former Title).
